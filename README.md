# Memory Match Buddy

A quick, clean card-matching game — flip tiles, remember what's where, and clear the board in as few moves as possible.

Memory Match Buddy is the classic "concentration" card game: a grid of face-down cards, each hiding a matching pair of icons. Flip two cards at a time to find matches, keep flipping until the whole board is cleared, and try to beat your own best move count and time. It tracks your moves, your clock, and your personal best for every difficulty level, and lets you pick up a saved game right where you left off.

## How to Play

1. **Pick a difficulty** from the toggle at the top left of the screen — Easy, Medium, Hard, or Expert. Your choice is remembered for next time.
2. **Click any card** to flip it face up and reveal the icon underneath.
3. **Click a second card.** If the two icons match, both cards flip open permanently and turn green to show they're solved. If they don't match, both cards briefly flash red, then flip back face-down after a short pause.
4. **Keep going** — flip pairs one at a time until every card on the board is matched.
5. When the last pair is found, a **"You matched everything!"** banner appears along with a burst of confetti, showing your final move count and time.

The board locks itself for a moment after a wrong guess so you always get a clear look at the mismatched pair before it flips back — no rushing your memory.

## Difficulty Levels

Each difficulty changes the size of the grid (and therefore how many pairs you need to remember):

| Difficulty | Grid Size | Pairs to Find |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | 4 × 4 | 8 pairs |
| Medium | 4 × 6 | 12 pairs |
| Hard | 6 × 6 | 18 pairs |
| Expert | 6 × 8 | 24 pairs |

Every card face is a food-themed emoji (pizza, fruit, desserts, and more), randomly shuffled and re-selected fresh at the start of every new game — so the exact set of icons you see, and their positions, is different each time.

Switching difficulty immediately deals a brand-new shuffled board — re-clicking the difficulty you're already on deals a fresh board at the same size too.

## Scoring & Tracking

The toolbar at the top of the board shows three live stats while you play:

- **Moves** — counts up by one every time you flip a *pair* of cards (a "move" = two card flips, match or not). Fewer moves means a more efficient memory.
- **Timer** — starts counting the moment you flip your very first card, and keeps running until you match the last pair.
- **Best** — your personal best move count for the currently selected difficulty, so you always have a target to beat.

### High Scores

Click the award/trophy icon in the top-right corner to open the **High Scores** panel. It lists your best result (moves and time) for each of the four difficulties side by side, so you can see your records across Easy, Medium, Hard, and Expert at a glance. A "Clear High Scores" button is available there if you ever want to wipe your records and start fresh (it asks for confirmation first, since it can't be undone).

A new best is only recorded if you finish with fewer moves than your previous record, or the same number of moves but a faster time — so ties in moves are broken by speed.

## Resuming & Sharing Progress

- **Automatic resume**: If you leave mid-game (close the tab, navigate away, etc.), Memory Match Buddy remembers your board, matched pairs, move count, and elapsed time. Next time you open the game, it will ask "We found a saved game on your system — do you want to resume from where you left off?" Choose **Yes** to continue exactly where you were, or **No** to discard it and start a new game.
- **Share Progress**: Click the paper-plane icon in the top-right corner at any time during a game to copy a special link to your clipboard. Sending that link to yourself or someone else (e.g., opening it on another device) will load the exact same board state — same difficulty, same matched cards, same move count and timer — so you can continue a game elsewhere or hand off a puzzle to someone.

## Visual & Celebration Effects

- Cards flip with a smooth 3D animation when revealed.
- A wrong pair gives a brief shake animation and a red highlight before flipping back down, making mismatches easy to notice.
- A correct match pulses green briefly to confirm the pair is locked in.
- Finishing the board triggers a full-screen confetti celebration alongside the completion banner.
- The board and cards resize responsively to fit smaller screens, so the game is comfortable to play on mobile as well as desktop.
- If your system is set to reduce motion, animations (flip, shake, pulse, confetti, banner) are automatically toned down.

## Settings

The gear icon in the top-right corner opens **Settings**, where you can:

- Switch between **Light** and **Dark** theme.
- Choose an **accent color** (Indigo, Blue, Green, Rose, Amber, or Purple) to personalize the look of buttons, highlights, and the active difficulty toggle.

These preferences are saved automatically and will still be applied the next time you open the game.

## Tips

- Try to build a mental map of the grid by position (e.g., "top-left corner, second row") rather than just the icon — it's easier to recall a location than to recall which of 24 possible icons was where.
- On Hard and Expert boards, focus on clearing one section of the grid at a time instead of jumping around — it reduces how much you have to hold in memory at once.
- Watch the **Best** stat as you play — if you're on pace to beat it, it can help you decide whether to play carefully (fewer moves) or quickly (better time) on your next match.
- Because moves only increase when a *second* card is flipped, there's no penalty for taking your time studying the first card of a pair before committing to your second guess.
- If you accidentally start a new game and want your progress back, check that you haven't cleared it — sharing your progress link to yourself first is a handy way to keep a backup of a board you don't want to lose.
